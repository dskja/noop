# NOOP v9.0.0 — Strand

The v5 hub redesign. NOOP is now organised around four everyday hubs — **Today**, **Insights**, **Health** and **Sources** — with the same structure on iPhone, Mac and Android. Everything underneath is the same offline, on-device app; only the navigation and information architecture changed.

Update from the Releases page, AltStore, or `brew upgrade` as usual.

## The big one: Strand hubs

- **Today** stays the dashboard: your recovery, strain, sleep and the live heart-rate curve.
- **Insights** is the home for *What Moves You*, Intelligence, Coach, Explore and Compare — every surface that helps you understand what drives your recovery.
- **Health** pulls together Live, Workouts, Stress, Breathe, Sleep, Trends, Lab Book and Rhythm. These are the body and vitals tools you reach for during the day.
- **Sources** is the index for Devices, imports, Data Sources, Backup & Sync, Fused Record, Automations, Alarms, Notifications, Settings, Support and Test Centre — everything that feeds data into NOOP or configures the app.

On **iOS** the floating glass tab bar now reads Today · Insights · Health · Sources. Sleep and Trends moved out of the tab bar and into Sources. The centre quick-action button still opens Live, Workout, Journal and Breathe.

On **macOS** the sidebar regrouped into Today, Insights, Health, Sources and Settings, with every destination still reachable and the search field still covering all of them.

On **Android** the glass bottom bar matches iOS, and the Sources page groups the full destination list the same way the iOS Sources tab does.

## WHOOP 5.0 / MG R22 deep data auto-applies

When the *noopWhoop5DeepData* experiment is enabled, NOOP now re-sends the 15-flag R22 unlock sequence automatically once per encrypted 5.0 / MG bond, right after the secure connect handshake finishes. Deep sensor streams come back without you tapping a button after every reconnect. The manual toggle in the device readout stays available for off-wrist or troubleshooting cases.

The sequence is guarded per-connection and reset on disconnect, so a fresh bond always gets a fresh application.

## What else is in this box

This release is intentionally a focused, non-empty milestone: the hub redesign is a large surface-area change, and it carries forward every 7.9.0 fix and improvement underneath. No behaviour changes to scoring, no new experiments, no empty release notes.
